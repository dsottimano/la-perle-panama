Source filenames are owner labels, not independent location verification. The site uses the coastal skyline, Amador and night views. No footage represents an individual listed property.

All web clips are silent H.264, 1280×720, 24fps, fast-start MP4. Hero: 10 seconds; remaining clips: 8 seconds. A 0.6-second end-to-start dissolve softens the loop seam. All posters are WebP and below 120 KB. The superseded Pexels clips and their posters have been removed.
