This 120 m² loft is in a restored colonial building in the heart of Casco Antiguo. Original nineteenth-century details, including timber beams, masonry walls and high ceilings, sit alongside contemporary interiors, two bedrooms, two bathrooms and an open kitchen.

The building has a rooftop terrace overlooking the bay and the modern city skyline. Restaurants, art galleries and specialty cafés are within walking distance.
