<p>Price per square metre is the single figure that best captures the pulse of a property market, and Panama City enters 2026 in notably solid shape. After several years absorbing accumulated inventory, the premium districts are showing sustained increases again, driven by local buyer demand and by a steady flow of foreign investment drawn to the stability of a dollarised economy.</p>
<p>ACOBIR's first-quarter 2026 figures confirm that there is no single city price, but clearly distinct markets: the Casco Antiguo leads on scarcity and heritage value, Punta Pacífica holds its premium for exclusivity and sea views, while San Francisco offers the best value-for-location for buyers looking to get into a central district. Below are the numbers worth having on the table before you buy.</p>
